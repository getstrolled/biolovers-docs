Visitors can leave short public comments on a profile. Comments are open to **everyone** — it isn't a Premium feature.

On a public page, a floating **chat-bubble button** sits in the bottom-right corner. Tapping it opens the comments panel, which lists the existing comments (newest first) and, if you're signed in, a box to post your own.

## Leave a comment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the comments panel">
    On the profile, tap the **chat-bubble button** in the bottom-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    You have to be **logged in** to post. Signed-out visitors see a **"Log in to leave a comment"** link instead of the box; they can still read existing comments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your comment (up to **200 characters** — there's a live counter) and tap **Send**. It appears at the top of the list right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Logged in** | You must be signed in to your own account to post. Reading is open to anyone. |
| **200 characters** | That's the hard limit per comment. The counter shows how many you have left. |
| **Once every 12 hours** | You can comment on the **same profile** at most once per 12 hours. Try again sooner and you'll see a "you can comment again in ~Xh" message. |

These limits are enforced on the server, so they apply no matter what.

## Turn comments off (profile owner)

Comments are **on by default**. To switch them off for your own profile:

1. Open the [Domain & URL panel](https://dashboard.biolovers.site/dashboard/edit/profile/url).
2. In the **Who can see it** section, untick **Allow comments on my profile**.

With comments off, the chat-bubble button disappears from your page and no one can post. Turn it back on any time from the same checkbox.

<Note>
Comments are also hidden automatically when a profile is **Hidden** (private). See [Domain & URL](/profile/domain-and-url) for the visibility modes.
</Note>

## Delete a comment

A trash button shows on comments you're allowed to remove:

- The **author** can delete their own comment.
- The **profile owner** can delete any comment on their page.

Deleting is immediate.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to comment?">
    Yes — you have to be logged in. Anyone can read comments, but posting requires a signed-in account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I post again?">
    There's a 12-hour cooldown per profile. If you commented on this profile in the last 12 hours, wait until the timer in the panel clears.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I comment on my own profile?">
    Yes. The 12-hour cooldown still applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the chat-bubble button on someone's page">
    The owner turned comments off, or the profile is private. There's nothing to do on your end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone left a comment I don't want">
    As the profile owner you can delete any comment on your page with the trash button. To stop new ones entirely, untick **Allow comments on my profile** on the [Domain & URL panel](https://dashboard.biolovers.site/dashboard/edit/profile/url). If a comment breaks the rules, report it via the [Discord](https://biolovers.site/discord) or the [contact form](https://biolovers.site/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
