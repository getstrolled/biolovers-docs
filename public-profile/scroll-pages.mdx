A scroll page is an **extra widget area below your main card**. Visitors swipe / scroll down to see it. Useful for splitting bio + portfolio + FAQ across distinct screens.

## When to use them

- Your main card is already busy and you want a "page 2".
- You want to dedicate a screen to your portfolio without crowding the bio.
- You're on mobile and want short, scannable sections instead of one long page.

## When NOT to use them

- If you only have 2–3 widgets, just put them on the main card.
- Visitors *will* miss content past the second screen — only put extra-credit content down there.

## Configuring

Open **Scroll pages** in the [Widgets & projects panel](https://dashboard.biolovers.site/dashboard/edit/pages/widgets). Each page has:

- A **number** — its position in the scroll order.
- An optional **title** shown above the page.

Click **Add scroll page** to make one, then send a widget to it with that widget's **Show on** dropdown. You can send a social integration to a page the same way, from the integration's **Show on** dropdown. You can have up to ten pages.

## Reordering

Each page row has **up and down arrows**. Use them to change a page's number:

- The number is the order visitors scroll through — page 1 shows first, then page 2, and so on.
- Move a page up to show it earlier, or down to show it later. The other pages shift to fill the gap. For example, moving page 4 up to position 2 pushes the old pages 2 and 3 down to 3 and 4.
- The widgets and integrations you placed on a page stay with it when you move it.

## Tips

- Put a **small "scroll for more" hint** (an arrow icon, the next-section heading) just above the fold so visitors know there's more.
- If your main card has a lot of links, dedicate **page 2** to long-form content (markdown + portfolio).
- Test on mobile — scroll pages feel different on phones than on a 1440 monitor.
