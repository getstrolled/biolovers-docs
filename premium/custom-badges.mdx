Buy slots at [biolovers.site/custom-badges](https://biolovers.site/custom-badges), then upload your designs in the [Badges panel](https://dashboard.biolovers.site/dashboard/edit/badges). See [Custom badges](/badges/custom-badges) for the design steps.

## Pricing

A custom badge slot costs **$3.99 USD** before discounts. Standard purchases have a volume discount of **5% for each extra slot after the first**, up to **20%**.

| Slots | Discount | Total | Approximate price per slot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0% | **$3.99** | $3.99 |
| 2 | 5% | **$7.58** | $3.79 |
| 3 | 10% | **$10.77** | $3.59 |
| 4 | 15% | **$13.57** | $3.39 |
| 5 | 20% | **$15.96** | $3.19 |

## Six slots for the price of five

Choose **5-for-6** to pay **$15.96** and receive **6 slots**. That is about **$2.66 per slot**. The bundle includes the five-slot volume discount.

## Optional badge with Premium

When buying [Lifetime Premium](/premium/what-premium-unlocks), the checkout can offer **one custom badge slot at half price**. The slot rounds to **$2.00**, so Premium at **$5.99** plus that optional slot totals **$7.99**.

This is an optional checkout offer. Standard slot purchases use the prices above.

## How to buy

1. Open [Custom badges checkout](https://biolovers.site/custom-badges).
2. Choose the standard quantity or **5-for-6** bundle.
3. Select **Purchase**, then **Card** or **Crypto**.
4. Review the total, accept the Terms and Refund policy, and complete payment.
5. Open the [Badges panel](https://dashboard.biolovers.site/dashboard/edit/badges) to create your designs after payment confirms.

Slots stack on your account. Deleting a custom badge returns its slot so you can use another design.

For purchases for another member, use [Gifting](/premium/gift). For refund eligibility, see [Refunds](/premium/refunds).
