Gift **Lifetime Premium** or **custom badge slots** to another Biolovers member. You pay; the upgrade is applied to their account.

## What you can gift

| Product | What they get |
|---------|---------------|
| **Premium** | Lifetime Premium on their account — same unlocks as buying for yourself. See [What Premium unlocks](/premium/what-premium-unlocks). |
| **Custom badges** | Extra custom badge slots they design with their own icon. See [Custom badges](/badges/custom-badges). Pick a bundle size on the gift page. |

You can't gift Verified or a donation tier from this page — those stay on their own checkout flows.

## How to gift

1. Sign in at <a href="https://dashboard.biolovers.site" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dashboard.biolovers.site</a> (gifting requires a signed-in buyer).
2. Open <a href="https://biolovers.site/gift" target="_blank" rel="noopener">biolovers.site/gift</a>.
3. Choose **Premium** or **Custom badges**.
4. Search the recipient by **username**. The page confirms they're eligible (e.g. they don't already own Premium if you're gifting Premium).
5. Accept the Terms and Refund policy, then pay with **card** (Stripe) or **crypto** (NOWPayments) — same rails as self-checkout.

When payment clears, the upgrade lands on their account automatically. They don't need to enter a code.

## Tips

- The recipient needs an existing account. Ask them to [sign up](/getting-started/sign-up) first if they don't have one.
- You can't gift Premium to yourself from this page — use the normal [Premium checkout](https://biolovers.site/premium).
- If the lookup says they already have Premium, pick **Custom badges** instead (or gift someone else).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Premium myself to gift?">
    No. Any signed-in account can buy a gift for someone else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will they get an email?">
    The upgrade is applied to their account as soon as payment succeeds. Point them at the dashboard if they don't notice right away.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I gift Verified?">
    Not from the gift page. They buy Verified themselves — see [Verified](/premium/verified).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the payment fails?">
    Nothing is applied until payment succeeds. Retry from the gift page, or see [Refunds](/premium/refunds) if money left your account and nothing landed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
